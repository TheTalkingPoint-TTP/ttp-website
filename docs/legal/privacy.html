Privacy statement.

How The Talking Point Group collects, processes and safeguards your personal information under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Policy owner
The Talking Point Group
Effective date
3 April 2024
Version
1.0 · last revised 3 April 2024
Section 01

Introduction

The Talking Point Group (Pty) Ltd (hereinafter referred to as "the Company") is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) as amended from time to time.

Section 02

Scope

This privacy statement applies to the personal information collected, processed, and stored by the Company in the course of its business activities. This includes but is not limited to being a Juristic Representative of a Financial Services Provider (FSP) and offering value-added services and products.

Section 03

Types of personal information collected

The Company may collect the following types of personal information:

  • We collect personal information directly from individuals or through authorised third parties, such as customers, employees, suppliers, and other stakeholders.
  • Personal information may include but is not limited to names, contact details, identification numbers, financial information, addresses and other relevant data necessary for our business activities.
  • Any other information necessary for the provision of value-added services and products.
Section 04

Purpose of processing

The Company processes personal information for the following purposes:

  • Provision of financial services as a Juristic Representative of an FSP.
  • Delivery of value-added services and products.
  • Processing of information for lawful and legitimate purposes related to our business operations, includes but not limited to:
    • Providing products or services requested by clients.
    • Managing client relationships, inquiries, and support.
    • Processing payments, invoices, and financial transactions.
    • Complying with legal and regulatory requirements.
    • Conducting marketing and promotional activities with consent.
    • Managing employee records, payroll, and HR processes.
    • Facilitating supplier relationships and procurement activities.
Section 05

Legal basis for processing

We process personal information based on lawful grounds as stipulated in POPIA, including the necessity for the performance of contracts, compliance with legal obligations, legitimate interests pursued by the Company or with the consent of the data subject where required.

Section 06

Data security and confidentiality

  • The Company employs reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.
  • Access to personal information is restricted to authorised personnel who require such access for legitimate business purposes.
Section 07

Data retention and disposal

  • The Company retains personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected unless retention is required by law or contractual obligations.
  • Personal information no longer needed is securely disposed of or anonymised to prevent unauthorised access or use.
Section 08

International data transfers

In the event of international data transfers, the Company ensures adequate safeguards and compliance with data protection regulations to protect the privacy and security of personal information.

Section 09

Data subject rights

  • Individuals have the right to access, rectify, or delete their personal information, object to processing, and withdraw consent where applicable, subject to legal and contractual requirements.
  • Requests regarding personal information can be submitted in writing to our designated Privacy Officer at info@thetalkingpoint.co.za.
Section 10

Privacy compliance

The Company is committed to regularly review and update our privacy practices, policies, and procedures to ensure compliance with POPIA and other relevant data protection laws.

Section 11

Data Protection Officer

For any inquiries regarding data protection, please contact our Data Protection Officer at info@thetalkingpoint.co.za.

Section 12

Changes to the privacy statement

The Company reserves the right to update this privacy statement to reflect changes in legal or business requirements. The updated statement will be made available on our website.

By using our services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this privacy statement.

Have a question?

For information requests, policy questions, or to exercise your rights under this policy, write to us. We'll respond in writing.
