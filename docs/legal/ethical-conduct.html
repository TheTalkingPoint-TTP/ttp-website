Ethical conduct policy.

The principles, responsibilities and reporting channels that guide how everyone at The Talking Point conducts business — with each other, with clients, and with the wider community.

Policy owner
The Talking Point Group
Effective date
3 April 2024
Version
1.0 · last revised 3 April 2024
Section 01

Purpose

At The Talking Point (TTP), we are committed to conducting our business with the highest standards of ethics, integrity, and transparency. This Ethical Conduct Policy outlines the principles and guidelines that all employees, contractors, and stakeholders must adhere to in their interactions and decision-making processes.

Section 02

Scope

The scope of this Ethical Conduct Policy is to:

  • Define the ethical standards and expectations for behaviour within TTP.
  • Promote a culture of honesty, fairness, and respect in all business dealings.
  • Provide guidance on identifying and addressing ethical dilemmas and conflicts of interest.
  • Ensure compliance with applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards.
Section 03

Core principles

TTP is guided by the following core principles of ethical conduct in respect of colleagues, clients, contractors, and stakeholders:

Integrity: Act honestly and ethically in all business activities, maintaining the highest standards of personal and professional integrity.

Respect: Treat all individuals with dignity, fairness, and respect, regardless of their background, beliefs, or position.

Transparency: Conduct business transparently, providing accurate and truthful information to stakeholders and avoiding deceptive or misleading practices.

Compliance: Adhere to all applicable laws, regulations, and internal policies, ensuring legal and regulatory compliance in all business operations.

Confidentiality: Safeguard confidential and proprietary information of TTP, clients, contractors, and stakeholders, and respect the privacy rights of individuals.

Section 04

Responsibilities

Management

Senior management and leaders are responsible for setting a positive example of ethical conduct, fostering a culture of integrity, and providing guidance and support to employees.

Employees and management

All employees are expected to familiarise themselves with this Ethical Conduct Policy, comply with its principles and guidelines, and report any unethical behaviour or violations observed.

Conflict of Interest: Avoid conflicts of interest that may compromise objectivity or impartiality in decision-making. Disclose any potential conflicts of interest to management or the appropriate authority.

Anti-Corruption: Prohibit bribery, corruption, kickbacks, and other unethical practices, whether involving public officials, private individuals, or business partners.

Fair Competition: Engage in fair and ethical competition, respecting the rights of competitors and refraining from unfair trade practices, price-fixing, or market manipulation.

Gifts and Entertainment: Exercise discretion and moderation when giving or receiving gifts, hospitality, or entertainment, ensuring they do not influence business decisions or create obligations.

Data Privacy: Respect and protect the privacy of personal and sensitive information, comply with data protection laws, and obtain consent for collecting and processing personal data.

Environmental Responsibility: Promote sustainable practices, minimize environmental impact, and comply with environmental laws and regulations.

Compliance with Laws and Regulations: All employees, contractors and representatives must comply with applicable laws and regulations in the countries where we operate. Ignorance of the law is not an excuse, and employees and representatives are encouraged to seek guidance when unsure about legal requirements.

Section 05

Reporting violations

Employees, representatives, contractors, and stakeholders are encouraged to report any suspected violations of this Ethical Conduct Policy or unethical behaviour to info@thetalkingpoint.co.za.

Reports may be made anonymously, and retaliation against individuals who report violations in good faith is strictly prohibited.

Section 06

Consequences of non-compliance

Violations of this Ethical Conduct Policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contractual relationship. Additionally, legal consequences may apply if violations involve criminal conduct or breach of laws.

Section 07

Review and approval

This Ethical Conduct Policy will be reviewed periodically to ensure its effectiveness, relevance, and alignment with evolving ethical standards, legal requirements, and industry best practices.

Have a question?

For information requests, policy questions, or to exercise your rights under this policy, write to us. We'll respond in writing.
