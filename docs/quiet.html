Chapter one · Ambient

Press play, and stay.

Eight nature loops, licensed to TTP through Envato Elements. Each one plays seamlessly for as long as you need — while you work, cook, or fall asleep. Volume lives in your device.

  1. ∞ Loop

    Steady rain.

    Slow, unbroken rain. No thunder, no talking, no ads. For work, sleep, or a hard afternoon.

  2. ∞ Loop

    Ocean at night.

    Slow surf on a calm beach. The kind of rhythm the nervous system remembers before you do.

  3. ∞ Loop

    Forest, at dawn.

    Wind through leaves, a slow bird call, occasional water. Somewhere else, for a moment.

  4. ∞ Loop · 4:58

    River, at night.

    Slow water over stones, distant crickets, one bird calling once. The kind of night that isn't lonely.

  5. ∞ Loop · 4:07

    Farm, first light.

    Dawn on a small farm. Distant rooster, wind through grass, one dog somewhere unbothered. Softer than a city morning.

  6. ∞ Loop · 4:25

    Flowing water.

    A steady river running clear over rocks. Nothing dramatic — just constant, patient movement in the background.

  7. ∞ Loop · 3:02

    Gentle water laps.

    Small waves lapping at a still edge. The softest of the eight — barely there, just enough to fill the silence.

  8. ∞ Loop · 4:29

    Nightingale, countryside.

    A single nightingale singing in the distance, soft countryside behind it. Slow, alive, unhurried — the sound of somewhere no one is in a rush.

Chapter two · Guided

Sit with someone's voice.

Curated guided practices from qualified teachers — distributed under CC‑BY‑NC via The Free Mindfulness Project. Grouped by what they do. Every piece plays in‑page.

01 · Breath

Mindfulness of breath.

Breath is always with you and always in the present. Come back to it when the mind is somewhere else.

  1. 3 Min

    Three-minute breathing.

    The shortest one. A tightly-timed reset when there isn't time for anything else.

    Free Mindfulness Project · CC‑BY‑NC
  2. 5 Min

    Five-minute breath.

    Gentle, unhurried breath work. A useful daily anchor — short enough to actually do, long enough to feel a difference.

    Life Happens · CC‑BY‑NC
  3. 11 Min

    Compassionate breath.

    Vidyamala Burch's self-compassion practice. Meet yourself with softness on a hard day.

    Vidyamala Burch · CC‑BY‑NC
02 · Body

Body scan.

Move your focus around the body, curious about what's there. Good after a long day, and better before sleep.

  1. 4 Min

    Four-minute body scan.

    Head to feet, quickly. Good for a lunch break or between meetings when the shoulders are up around the ears.

    Still Mind · CC‑BY‑NC
  2. 6 Min

    Release tension.

    Vidyamala Burch (Breathworks) walks you through letting go of held stress in the body. Warm voice, no jargon.

    Vidyamala Burch · CC‑BY‑NC
  3. 23 Min

    Body scan for sleep.

    Longer, slower — from head to feet with permission to drift off along the way. For 3am on a hard night.

    UCSD Center for Mindfulness · CC‑BY‑NC
  4. 47 Min

    Deep body scan · long.

    Forty-seven minutes for when you want to give it real time. Sleep-length. Drift off if you need to.

    UCSD Center for Mindfulness · CC‑BY‑NC
03 · Sitting

Sitting meditation.

Longer practice with breath as the anchor, expanding into sound and the whole body. For when you have the time.

  1. 12 Min

    Breath, sound, body.

    A three-part sitting practice from the UCLA Mindful Awareness Research Center. Start with breath, land in sound, finish in the body.

    UCLA MARC · CC‑BY‑NC
04 · Silence

Self-guided practice.

For when you want to sit in silence. A single bell at the start, another at the end. Bring your own practice.

  1. 10 Min

    Ten minutes with just bells.

    Silence, three gentle bells — one at the start, one in the middle, one at the end. No voice.

    Free Mindfulness Project · CC‑BY‑NC
