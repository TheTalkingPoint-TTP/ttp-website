Tonight·Six small steps·Make it to morning

You don't have to fix anything. You just have to make it to morning.

  1. 01 Reach out

    Tell one person.

    Anyone. A friend. A sibling. ListenUp on WhatsApp. The line above. You don't have to explain — just “I'm not okay tonight.”

  2. 02 Reduce access to means

    Put space between you and means of harm.

    Give pills, ropes, knives, firearms to someone else for tonight. Lock them in the car. Hand them to a neighbour. Just for tonight.

  3. 03 Refuel

    Eat something small.

    Toast. A banana. Yoghurt. Blood sugar matters more than you think when everything feels final.

  4. 04 Find safe ground

    Sit somewhere safe.

    A friend's couch. The lounge with the TV on. A 24/7 garage forecourt. Not alone in the dark if you don't have to be.

  5. 05 Postpone

    Don't decide anything tonight.

    Big decisions — leaving, ending, signing, sending — none of them have to happen tonight. They can wait. You can wait.
